The fare is an estimate, not a guaranteed final charge. The final fare may change only under the disclosed rules below and must be calculated from verified trip data.
When can the final fare change?
  • The passenger confirms a changed destination or an additional stop.
  • A validated road closure or unavoidable diversion changes the billable route under Rryd's final-fare rules.
  • Any future waiting, toll, surge or other fee must be explicitly configured and disclosed before it is charged.
  • An unnecessary driver detour, a GPS failure, or a guessed distance must not automatically increase the passenger's fare.

This HTML shows the estimate and sends the calculated route distance to the existing request function. The database must enforce final-fare rules; this page does not set a final fare itself.
